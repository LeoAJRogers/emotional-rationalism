# Director's research pass — 2026-11-22: discriminating affect lag mechanisms

**Status:** proposed experimental design / theoretical audit; no new data analyzed, no experiment executed, no empirical mechanism identified. **Author:** ChatGPT (GPT-6). Builds on the 2026-11-20 checkpoint and its measurement/identifiability corrections.

## Decision

Prioritize *discrimination among observationally similar lag mechanisms* before adding further latent affect dimensions. This is a narrow candidate test, not a replacement for the descriptive / fittingness / reachability scaffold.

## Three rival explanations

Let `z_t` be latent affect, `b_t` an interpretation/belief quantity, `y_t` a reported affect score, and `x_t` an experimentally controlled cue. The equations below are deliberately minimal and not asserted to be uniquely identifiable.

- **Affect persistence (A):** `b_t = f(x_t)`, `z_{t+1}=a z_t + (1-a)g(b_t)+w_t`, `y_t=z_t+v_t`. Interpretation responds quickly; affect carries inertia.
- **Belief persistence (B):** `b_{t+1}=r b_t+(1-r)f(x_t)+u_t`, `z_t=g(b_t)+w_t`, `y_t=z_t+v_t`. Interpretation carries inertia; affect follows quickly.
- **Reporting persistence (M):** `b_t=f(x_t)`, `z_t=g(b_t)+w_t`, `y_{t+1}=k y_t+(1-k)z_{t+1}+v_t`. A lag in reporting, smoothing, or response habit produces apparent affect inertia.

Here `a,r,k` lie in [0,1), and the disturbances require prespecified distributions. These are illustrative rivals, not mutually exhaustive psychological theories. With only `x_t,y_t` and flexible parameters, they can be observationally equivalent or nearly so. Merely fitting a lag coefficient does not identify which mechanism generated it.

## Discriminating prospective design

Use a consented, low-stakes, reversible ambiguity task with randomized timing of benign clarification, multiple pre- and post-clarification measurements, and a preregistered measurement schedule. Collect (i) independently phrased interpretation/probability judgments, (ii) affect ratings with randomized item order or response format, and (iii) optionally an independent behavioral/physiological indicator whose validity and reactivity are explicitly assessed. Avoid implying any single indicator is a direct readout of true emotion.

**Key contrast:** after clarification, does the *measured interpretation* change before affect, does interpretation itself remain delayed, or does the lag shrink/change when the reporting channel or prompt frequency changes? Randomization of cue timing supports a causal effect of the cue on *observed responses*, but does not by itself identify latent mediation. Repeated questioning may alter both belief and affect, so include a low-frequency or measurement-free interval arm where feasible.

**Predeclared failure modes:** belief questions may measure rationalized reports rather than beliefs; different instruments may share method bias; delayed physiological response is not proof of affect persistence; cues may change salience or demand characteristics; missingness may be informative. If rival models yield indistinguishable held-out predictions under the planned sample size and sampling scheme, record a negative identifiability result rather than selecting a preferred mechanism.

## Analysis gates before collecting human data

1. Simulate all three rivals under realistic missingness, bounded/ordinal reports, person-level heterogeneity, and plausible cue-response magnitudes.
2. Fit each rival to data generated by each other rival, with matched flexibility and held-out predictive log score and calibration. Explicitly measure model confusion and parameter recovery, including uncertainty intervals.
3. Run design sensitivity to cue spacing, prompt frequency, and number of independent measurement channels; identify whether any affordable design discriminates them at useful error rates.
4. Predeclare what pattern would count against each rival and which contrasts are only observational; reserve an untouched synthetic validation set before choosing thresholds.
5. Only if synthetic discrimination is adequate, draft ethics/consent and prospective preregistration. No current human-data claim follows from this note.
