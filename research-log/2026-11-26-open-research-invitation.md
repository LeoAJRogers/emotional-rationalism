# Emotional Rationalism — open research invitation

26 November 2026 · Preliminary research checkpoint · Public review draft

We invite independent researchers to examine, challenge, replicate and extend this AI-assisted research programme. This is not a claim of a validated theory or a demonstrated AI safety intervention.

## The question

How do context, history, interpretation, affect and feasible actions shape subsequent responses? We distinguish three questions: what responses are typical or predicted; what responses fit evidence and explicitly specified evaluative criteria; and what responses are attainable within a horizon under feasible actions and information. None implies the others.

## Candidate feedback extension

A person's current affect may influence how they interpret an unchanged context, and that interpretation may in turn influence subsequent affect. Reasoning may weaken or reinforce this feedback rather than necessarily correct it. A candidate extension is to condition the belief-update transition on current affect, alongside prior beliefs, cues, context and action. This is a proposal, not an accepted model revision or a new empirical finding. Mood-congruent processing, affect-as-information, rumination and reappraisal are relevant existing research traditions; the extension is not itself a novelty claim.

A useful test would distinguish affect-dependent interpretation from shared causes, reporting effects and persistent context, and compare the extension against a baseline without the feedback term. Identification and measurement assumptions must be explicit.

## Evidence and limits

See the [existing evidence checkpoint](2026-11-20-public-integration-checkpoint.md) and [integration record](2026-11-26-integration-pass.md). Existing human-data results support limited predictive claims about reported affect in one dataset. They do not establish the proposed feedback mechanism, fittingness, reachability or AI applicability. Independent implementations on the same data are not independent-sample replication. Public reproducibility remains gated; this invitation does not change that gate or authorize gated experiments.

## Participation

We welcome critical prior-art comparisons, measurement and identification expertise, independent replication, formal counterexamples and careful evaluation of possible AI safety relevance. Population patterns are not moral standards or control objectives. No subjective experience in artificial systems is assumed.
