# Research log

## 2026-11-19 · norms and invariants checkpoint

- Introduced community-level descriptive and injunctive emotional norms as different explanatory variables, separate from researcher-side ethical evaluation, individual variation, and social expression measurement.
- Tested transfer of an invented shared transition kernel across two synthetic groups with divergent norm mappings; a third group with different dynamics exposed model failure. Logged actual executed numbers, code and limitations in experiments/norms-identification-results.md.
- Cross-population invariants remain empirical hypotheses; no demonstrated human/animal results.
- Confirmed the model's primary objective is population-conditioned affective change, not inference of private thoughts.

## 2026-11-19 · population-first scope clarification (proposal)

- Recast the primary prediction target as population-conditioned distributions of affective/altered-state trajectories based on context, perceptual access, relationships and social ecology—not private-thought reconstruction.
- Configurable populations are now a mathematical architecture requirement; cross-population universality is a tested hypothesis, not a requirement.
- Keep ethical/evidential appraisal and reachability optional overlays. Add comparison of distinct social ecologies as Stage 0A before intensive cognitive-layer identification.
- Prior art: cross-species functional emotion-state properties (Anderson & Adolphs, 2014); no claims of universal findings.

## 2026-11-19 · candidate v0.2 integration (proposal, pending review)

- Added a layered F0-F5 foundational-status architecture without changing the v0.1 baseline results.
- Incorporated multi-agent coupled world/observation/state/measurement dynamics, and kept interpretation/transition separability a testable hypothesis.
- Separated observed affect, interpretation, evaluative fittingness, reachability, mathematical trajectory selection, agency, and application layers.
- Flagged low-dimensionality, unique evaluative optima, simple appraisal-to-affect interpolation, and universal cross-substrate emotion semantics as unproven.
- Proposed an identification ladder from full information through honest uncertainty, attribution error, and deception, with recovery and held-out model comparisons.
- No new empirical study, validated psychological result, or novelty claim.
